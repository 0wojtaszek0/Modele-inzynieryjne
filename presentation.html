Application Overview

EGEA Suspension Tester is a professional engineering application for analyzing vehicle suspension dampers according to the EGEA (European Gauge of Automotive) standard. The application provides:

  • Complete 2-DOF Quarter-Car Model simulation
  • Real-time data import and analysis (CSV format)
  • Advanced signal processing with Butterworth filtering
  • Automatic minimum phase shift calculation
  • EGEA standard parameter validation
  • Professional visualization and reporting

Technical Model

Quarter-Car System Equations

The 2-DOF Quarter-Car Model consists of two masses connected by springs and dampers:

$$m\ddot{x}_m = -(k_M + k_m)x_m + k_M x_M - (c_M + c_m)\dot{x}_m + c_M \dot{x}_M + k_m z(t) + c_m \dot{z}(t)$$
$$M\ddot{x}_M = k_M x_m - k_M x_M + c_M \dot{x}_m - c_M \dot{x}_M$$

Platform Excitation

The test platform follows a predefined frequency sweep:

$$z(t) = -d \cos(\theta(t))$$

where d is the excitation amplitude (typically 6 mm peak-to-peak).

Key Performance Indicators

Minimum Phase Shift (phi_min)

The primary indicator of damper performance is the minimum phase shift during the measurement range (18-6 Hz):

$$\phi_{min} = \arccos\left(\frac{F_{min} - \bar{F}}{\Delta F/2}\right)$$

Where:

  • Fmin: Minimum force in measurement range
  • F̄: Average force = (Fmax + Fmin)/2
  • ΔF: Force amplitude = (Fmax - Fmin)/2
phi_min < 30°
Damper may be too stiff or worn
30° ≤ phi_min ≤ 60°
Good damper performance
phi_min > 60°
Damper may be too soft or over-extended

EUSAMA Index

$$\text{EUSAMA} = \frac{F_{min}}{F_{st}} \times 100\%$$
  • EUSAMA > 50%: Damper is functional
  • EUSAMA < 50%: Damper may be defective

Relative Force Amplitude (RFA_max)

$$\text{RFA}_{max} = \frac{\max(\Delta F)}{F_{st}} \times 100\%$$

Indicates the maximum force oscillation relative to static load.
